Server plans

Monthly or yearly subscription, cancel anytime. Basic unlocks the full Pyxie profile (nickname, photo, banner and bio) and the dashboard colors; Premium adds the store status, up to 10 cyclic messages and the AI Oracle. Yearly gives you 2 months free, and the Store Studio comes as a R$ 3 extra. If the month is not paid, the perks are paused and the owner gets a DM — nothing is deleted.
